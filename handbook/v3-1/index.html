7. Paid usage rights (v3.1)

The term starts on the first delivery date in the ad account's own time zone.

Extensions are bought in 15-day blocks from the day after the term ends.

The rights budget is $8,000 a month.

An agent can agree a monthly rate up to 30% below the contract by email.

An exclusive deal with another brand only blocks an extension if it starts before the term ends.

GBP spend is converted at the finance close rate.
